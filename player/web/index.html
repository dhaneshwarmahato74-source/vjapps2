Photosensitive seizure warning

This software produces rapid flashing light. Flashing can trigger seizures in people with photosensitive epilepsy – often without any previous history.

  • Safe mode is ON by default: flash rate is limited to 3 per second and brightness to 70 %.
  • Display a visible warning to your audience, and avoid red-dominant full-field flashes and high-contrast patterns above 3 Hz.
  • Esc or B is the instant blackout. The blackout button is always at the top of the screen.

Videos change only on the active source: beats in Audio mode, console pulse (channel set in Settings) or flash count in Tiger mode, buttons / , . in Manual mode. Clips are muted; files stay in this browser session only.

Look & timing

Console and audio modes take colour and timing from their own source; brightness, duration and decay always apply.

Strobe colours

Tap a colour to switch it ON / OFF. Audio mode and the K key only use colours that are ON – an OFF colour never appears. At least one stays on.
